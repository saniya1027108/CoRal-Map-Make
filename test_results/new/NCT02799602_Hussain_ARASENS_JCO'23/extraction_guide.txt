**SECTION 4: DATA ORGANIZATION**

*   **Patient Stratification:** Patients are primarily stratified by:
    1.  **Disease Volume:** High Volume vs. Low Volume (based on CHAARTED criteria). These are mutually exclusive.
    2.  **Disease Risk:** High Risk vs. Low Risk (based on LATITUDE criteria). These are mutually exclusive.
    3.  **Other Subgroups (within volume/risk categories):** Age, ECOG PS, Gleason score at initial diagnosis, Metastasis stage at initial diagnosis (de novo/recurrent), Metastasis stage at screening (M1a/M1b/M1c), Serum PSA level, Serum ALP level, Race, Geographical region.

*   **Do subgroups overlap?**
    *   Yes, the "Disease Volume" stratification (High/Low Volume) and "Disease Risk" stratification (High/Low Risk) are independent. A patient can be in "High Volume" and "High Risk", or "High Volume" and "Low Risk", etc. Therefore, patient counts for "High Volume" and "High Risk" should NOT be summed to get a total.
    *   Within each stratification (e.g., High Volume vs. Low Volume), the subgroups are mutually exclusive.
    *   Within the "Metastasis stage at screening" (Table 1), M1a, M1b, and M1c are mutually exclusive.
    *   Within the "Gleason score at initial diagnosis" (Table 1), <8 and ≥8 are mutually exclusive.
    *   For AEs (Table 3), Grade 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5 are mutually exclusive and sum to "Any AE". "Serious AE" is a subset of "Any AE" and can overlap with any grade.
    *   For subsequent therapies (Table A1), patients can receive multiple therapies, so individual therapy counts are not mutually exclusive.

**SECTION 5: AGGREGATION RULES**

*   **Total Patients (Efficacy):**
    *   Darolutamide arm: 651 (Figure 1)
    *   Placebo arm: 654 (Figure 1)
    *   Overall: 1305 (Figure 1)
*   **Total Patients (Safety):**
    *   Darolutamide arm: 652 (Table 3)
    *   Placebo arm: 650 (Table 3)
    *   Overall: 1302 (Table 3)
    *   *Note:* The discrepancy (1305 vs 1302) is explained in Table 3 footnote 'c'.
*   **Disease Volume Subgroups:**
    *   High Volume (Darolutamide): 497 (Table 1)
    *   Low Volume (Darolutamide): 154 (Table 1)
    *   Total Darolutamide (High + Low Volume): 497 + 154 = 651 (Matches total efficacy N)
    *   High Volume (Placebo): 508 (Table 1)
    *   Low Volume (Placebo): 146 (Table 1)
    *   Total Placebo (High + Low Volume): 508 + 146 = 654 (Matches total efficacy N)
*   **Disease Risk Subgroups:**
    *   High Risk (Darolutamide): 452 (Table 1)
    *   Low Risk (Darolutamide): 199 (Table 1)
    *   Total Darolutamide (High + Low Risk): 452 + 199 = 651 (Matches total efficacy N)
    *   High Risk (Placebo): 460 (Table 1)
    *   Low Risk (Placebo): 194 (Table 1)
    *   Total Placebo (High + Low Risk): 460 + 194 = 654 (Matches total efficacy N)
*   **Overlapping Subgroups:**
    *   **DO NOT SUM** patient counts from "High Volume" and "High Risk" columns, as these are independent stratifications and patients can belong to both.
    *   For "Metastasis stage at screening" (Table 1), M1a, M1b, and M1c are mutually exclusive. Summing them gives the total number of patients with known metastasis stage at screening.
    *   For "Gleason score at initial diagnosis" (Table 1), "<8" and "≥8" are mutually exclusive. Summing them gives the total number of patients with known Gleason score.
    *   For "ECOG PS" (Table 1), "0" and "1" are mutually exclusive. Summing them gives the total number of patients with known ECOG PS.
    *   For "Subsequent Life-Prolonging Systemic Antineoplastic Therapy" (Table A1), individual therapies (e.g., Abiraterone acetate, Enzalutamide) are NOT mutually exclusive. Patients may have received more than one. The row "Patients with subsequent life-prolonging systemic antineoplastic therapy" represents the total count of unique patients who received *at least one* subsequent therapy.

**SECTION 6: SPECIAL WARNINGS**

*   **Missing Data:** Always use `null` for missing data points, never infer or calculate a zero unless an explicit aggregation rule or context from the paper dictates it (e.g., if a category is explicitly stated as "0"). For example, if a median is "NE" (Not Estimable), record as `null`.
*   **Subgroup Overlap:** Be mindful that "Disease Volume" subgroups (High/Low Volume) and "Disease Risk" subgroups (High/Low Risk) are distinct stratifications. Do not combine data across these main subgroup types unless explicitly instructed for a specific aggregate field.
*   **Race/Region Data:** Table 1 provides race and geographical region data. If a specific race or region is not listed for a particular arm/subgroup, it means there were no patients in that category for that group.
*   **M1a/M1b/M1c Definition:** Note Table 1 footnote 'e' regarding one patient categorized as M1b in the high-volume subgroup despite having only lymph node metastasis. This indicates that the M1 categories are based on detailed tumor data, not just the M1a/M1b/M1c labels alone.
*   **AE Exposure Adjustment:** For "Most common AEs" in Table 3, EAIR (Exposure-Adjusted Incidence Rate) is provided. However, footnote 'e' states "Exposure adjustment is not applicable, as the majority of AEs occurred during concomitant docetaxel treatment." Extract the "No. (%)" values primarily, and note the EAIR if the column is present, but be aware of this caveat.
*   **Subsequent Therapy Denominators:** For Table A1, the denominators for percentages are explained in footnote 'c': "the number of patients who entered active or long-term follow-up, plus one patient who did not enter follow-up but received subsequent therapy." This is not simply the total N for the arm.
*   **Overall Survival (OS) and Time to CRPC (Figures 2 & 3):** The Kaplan-Meier curves show "No. of patients at risk" at various time points. Extract the initial N at time 0 for each curve, and the HR (95% CI) and Median (95% CI) as presented in the figure legends.
